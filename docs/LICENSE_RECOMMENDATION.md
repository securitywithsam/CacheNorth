# CacheNorth original-code licensing decision

**Current decision (November 2026):** Keep commercial rights reserved. The repository's [LICENSE](../LICENSE) grants limited personal, noncommercial early-access use of official builds. This is a proprietary, source-available approach, **not** an open-source license.

The owner intends to consider paid distribution later. A permissive license such as MIT or Apache-2.0 would allow third parties to commercially redistribute the licensed original code, including after the owner changes policy. No such grant has been issued.

If a commercial product is launched, separately define end-user licensing, support/refund terms, and third-party component notices. This proprietary notice does **not** override the independent GPL/AGPL/CC/ODbL and other licenses of bundled materials.

The iFixit archive is CC BY-NC-SA, and its use in a paid CacheNorth bundle must be licensed separately or omitted. See [Content Licenses](CONTENT_LICENSES.md) and [Third-party Licenses](THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES.md).

This is a practical rights-preservation choice, not legal advice. Consult a qualified licensing attorney before selling a combined third-party binary/content package.
